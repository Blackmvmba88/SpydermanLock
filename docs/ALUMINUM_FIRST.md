# Aluminum-first material strategy

SpydermanLock Mk I is designed around machined aluminum as the dominant visible and structural material.

## Preferred alloys

- 6061-T6: default for plate, rotor, lever and prototype receiver/cam
- 7075-T6: optional for a higher-strength rotor, lever or cam where wear/load justifies it
- 304/316 stainless steel: preferred for shaft, pins and high-wear strike surfaces
- Polycarbonate/acrylic: diffuser only

## Surface finish

Recommended production finish:

1. CNC or mill to final geometry
2. deburr and edge-break
3. bead-blast or fine brushed finish
4. anodize
5. laser-etch state marks if desired

The goal is a cold, dense, mechanical feel rather than a plastic prop feel.

## Functional exceptions

Not every component should be aluminum. The shaft, pins, bushings and high-contact wear surfaces may use stainless steel, bronze or engineering polymer where tribology demands it.
