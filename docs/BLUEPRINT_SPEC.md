# SpydermanLock Mk I — Blueprint Specification v0.2

## Material baseline

The visual and structural language is aluminum-first.

- Front plate: 6061-T6 aluminum
- Rotary hub: 6061-T6 aluminum
- Lever: 6061-T6 aluminum
- Shaft: stainless steel
- Rear cam: 6061-T6 prototype; 7075-T6 or stainless for higher wear
- Receiver: aluminum prototype; stainless strike insert recommended for long service
- Diffuser: translucent polycarbonate

## Core dimensions

- Front plate: 84 x 64 x 4 mm
- Rotor: 48 mm diameter x 10 mm
- Lever: 42 x 16 x 14 mm
- Shaft: 10 mm diameter
- Cam: 42 x 14 x 4 mm
- Receiver: 75 x 24 x 2 mm
- Receiver slot: 38 x 12 mm
- Target engagement: 18 mm
- Rotation: 0 to 90 degrees
- Triple-module center spacing: 95 mm

## Manufacturing intent

Machine the front-facing parts from billet where possible. Break all exposed edges, retain crisp industrial geometry, and prefer bead-blasted or brushed anodized aluminum over painted plastic.

The rotating hub should be the tactile centerpiece: low play, positive end stops, and a deliberate detent at 0 and 90 degrees.

## Prototype tolerances

- Plate outline: +/-0.3 mm
- Shaft bore: +0.1 / +0.3 mm
- Rotor axial play: 0.2-0.5 mm
- Cam/receiver working clearance: 1-2 mm

## Validation gate

Do not freeze CAD until one module passes 100 manual lock/unlock cycles without loosening, binding, or losing receiver alignment.
